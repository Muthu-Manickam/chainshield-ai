# SOP-05 Customer Notification for Delayed Orders

> Team-authored, illustrative procedure for the ChainShield demo.

## Purpose
Customers should hear about a delay from us before they discover it. This procedure sets when and how we notify customers of delayed orders, and what compensation may be offered.

## Roles
- **Logistics Analyst**: drafts and sends standard notifications.
- **Account Manager**: contacts key customers directly.
- **Operations Supervisor**: approves compensation above the analyst limit.

## When to notify
Notify the customer when a delay of 24 hours or more is confirmed, or when the new delivery date cannot be confirmed within 24 hours of the incident. Do not wait for a final answer from the carrier if the original date is already at risk.

## Time limits
| Customer tier | Contact method | Deadline from confirmed delay |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | Phone call by Account Manager, then email | 2 hours |
| Standard | Email using template | 4 hours |
| Small / online orders | Automated email or status page update | 8 hours |

## Steps
1. Confirm the facts: order ID, original date, new estimated date and cause. Use only verified information from the carrier (SOP-04) and route status (SOP-01).
2. Choose the template. Include what happened in plain words, the new estimated date or when we will next update, and what we are doing about it.
3. Do not state a cause that has not been confirmed. Do not blame the carrier by name.
4. Send the message and log the time and channel in the order record.
5. Update the customer every 24 hours until the order is delivered, or sooner if the date changes by more than 12 hours.
6. After delivery, send a short confirmation with the final delivery time.

## Compensation (illustrative)
| Delay | Option | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| 24 to 72 hours | Apology, shipping fee waiver up to $100 | Analyst |
| Over 72 hours | Credit up to $500 | Operations Supervisor |
| Over 7 days or order value over $20,000 | Custom remedy | Operations Manager |

## Messages for critical items
If the delayed goods are medicine, food or other time-critical items, the Account Manager calls the customer first and offers alternatives such as partial delivery or local sourcing, using SOP-06 to cost the options.

## Records
Log all messages, credits and approvals for the review in SOP-08. Review customer complaints weekly to improve templates.
