# SOP-04 Carrier Escalation Contacts and Order

> Team-authored, illustrative procedure for the ChainShield demo.

## Purpose
This procedure sets out who to contact at a carrier, and in what order, when a shipment is delayed, a route is unclear or a carrier is not responding. Contact details are held in the carrier register; the numbers shown here are placeholders.

## Roles
- **Logistics Analyst**: makes the first and second contacts.
- **Operations Supervisor**: makes third-level contact.
- **Vendor Manager**: owns the commercial relationship and handles final escalation.

## Escalation ladder
Each level has a response deadline. If the deadline passes without a useful answer, move to the next level.

| Level | Contact | Deadline | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Carrier dispatcher or control desk | 2 hours | Phone, then email |
| L2 | Carrier account manager | 4 hours from L1 | Phone and email |
| L3 | Carrier regional operations director | 8 hours from L2 | Phone, Supervisor places the call |
| L4 | Carrier commercial head, with the ChainShield Vendor Manager | 24 hours from L3 | Meeting or call, written follow-up |

Placeholder numbers for the demo: dispatcher desk +1-555-0101, account manager +1-555-0102, regional director +1-555-0103.

## Steps
1. Confirm the shipment details before calling: order ID, bill of lading or waybill number, origin, destination, last known position.
2. Contact L1 and ask three questions: where is the cargo, what is the new expected delivery time, and what caused the delay.
3. Log the time, person and answer. If the answer is vague, or no answer arrives within the deadline, go to L2.
4. When escalating, state the order value, the customer impact and the deadline in the first sentence.
5. Move straight to L3 for any shipment worth more than $50,000, any perishable load, or any order for a Platinum customer, skipping L1 and L2 only if the carrier has already failed to respond to an earlier request in the last 24 hours.
6. Use L4 only after consulting the Operations Manager. L4 contact may lead to a carrier change, which only the Operations Manager may approve.

## Out-of-hours
Outside business hours use the carrier emergency line. The Analyst on call may contact L1 and L2. Calls to L3 and L4 wait until the Supervisor or Manager is available, except when cargo is perishable or a safety risk exists.

## Records
Keep every contact record in the incident file. A carrier that misses two L1 deadlines in a month is flagged to the Vendor Manager for performance review.
