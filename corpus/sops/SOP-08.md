# SOP-08 Incident Review and Approval Policy

> Team-authored, illustrative procedure for the ChainShield demo.

## Purpose
This policy states which roles may approve which actions during an incident, and how each incident is reviewed afterwards. It brings together the limits used in SOP-02, SOP-03, SOP-05, SOP-06 and SOP-07.

## Roles
- **Logistics Analyst**: investigates, recommends and takes low-value actions.
- **Operations Supervisor**: approves mid-value actions and reviews analyst work.
- **Operations Manager**: approves high-value actions, carrier changes and policy exceptions.
- **Director of Operations**: approves actions above $100,000 and signs off major incident reviews.

## Approval matrix
| Action | Analyst | Supervisor | Operations Manager |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shipment hold | Up to $10,000 (notify Supervisor) | $10,001 to $50,000 | Over $50,000 |
| Expedite or re-route extra cost | Up to $2,000 | $2,001 to $10,000 | Over $10,000 |
| Alternate port diversion extra cost | Not allowed | Up to $25,000 | Over $25,000 |
| Customer credit | Up to $100 | Up to $500 | Over $500 |
| Warehouse reopening | Not allowed | Approves | Required for Red sites |
| Stock write-off | Not allowed | Up to $10,000 | Over $10,000 |
| Switch to a different carrier | Not allowed | Recommends | Approves |

Any single incident whose combined spend exceeds $100,000 goes to the Director of Operations.

## Rules
1. A person cannot approve an action they proposed above their own limit.
2. If the approver is unavailable, the next role up may approve. Never use a lower role.
3. Urgent safety actions may be taken first and approved within 2 hours.
4. Every approval is recorded with time, name and amount.

## Incident review
Every incident rated Medium or higher is reviewed within 5 business days of closure. Major incidents, with spend above $50,000 or a customer impact above 7 days, are reviewed within 10 business days with the Director.

### Review steps
1. The Analyst compiles the timeline: first alert, first action, each approval, closure.
2. Compare actions against SOP-01 to SOP-07 and note any deviations.
3. Compare actual cost with the quoted cost and the estimated avoided loss.
4. Identify what could have been spotted earlier, such as an alert that was missed.
5. The Supervisor signs the review; the Manager signs it for major incidents.
6. Actions to improve are logged with an owner and a due date within 30 days.

## Records
Reviews are kept for 3 years. Frequent deviations from the matrix are reported monthly to the Director.
